The facts

What it is
An AI coach and companion for veterans, first responders, active-duty service members, and the people who love them. Text or voice, any hour. Coach Jeff is not a therapist and not a crisis line.
Founder
Rusty Humphries, a nationally syndicated radio host on 250+ affiliates and a 17-year member of the Talkers Magazine list of America’s 100 most important talk show hosts. His father was killed in action in Vietnam on January 26, 1969.

Paste-ready paragraph

Use it as written, or cut it down. Please keep the line that says what Coach Jeff is not.

Coach Jeff is an AI coach and companion for veterans, first responders, and active-duty service members, and the people who love them. Available any hour by text or voice, he remembers you, gives you some grief, and is good company. He is not a therapist and not a crisis line. Coach Jeff was built by Rusty Humphries, a nationally syndicated radio host whose father was killed in action in 1969, and launches Veterans Day, November 11, 2026. HelpAVet.US, a separate sponsorship program, funds access for veterans who cannot afford it.

Using these

  • The name is two words: Coach Jeff. Never just “Jeff.”
  • Describe him accurately: an AI coach and companion. Not a therapist, a clinician, or a crisis service.
  • If your story touches on suicide or crisis, please include 988, and for veterans and service members, press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line.
  • Leave the artwork alone: no recoloring, stretching, cropping, or added effects.
  • Give logos room, and use the transparent files on dark or colored backgrounds.
