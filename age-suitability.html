Content This App Contains

Coach Jeff handles real conversations about real things — including combat trauma, PTSD, grief, anger, and the weight veterans carry long after they come home. The app integrates directly with the 988 Veterans Crisis Line for users who need immediate support. Conversations may involve references to suicidal ideation in the context of crisis prevention and support.

Jeff speaks like a veteran — direct and honest, and conversations may occasionally include profanity when it comes up naturally. It's not a constant feature of how he talks, but it's part of how he connects with the people he's built for.

This app is intended for users 16 and older. The subject matter — combat trauma, grief, crisis-related content, and occasional mature language — isn't appropriate for younger children. If you're under 16, this app is not for you.

This app is not a medical device. Coach Jeff is not intended to diagnose or treat any medical condition and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, call 988 or 911.
