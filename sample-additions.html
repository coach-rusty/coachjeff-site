The Promise

He's got four rules.
He doesn't break them.

01
He talks about everything
Movies, your team, sports, politics, your truck, your marriage — whatever's on your mind is what he wants to talk about. No agenda. No steering you toward "the real issue." Just a friend who's actually interested.
Your world. Your conversation.
02
He never forgets
You don't have to catch him up. He knows what you talked about last week, what you told him six months ago. The more you talk, the more he understands exactly who you are and what you need.
He gets to know you. That's the point.
03
He never tells a soul
What you say to Jeff stays with Jeff. Period. No records shared. No one else reads it. He's not going to tell your wife, your CO, or anyone else. What's said here stays here — always.
Vault-level privacy. No exceptions.
04
He never burns out
0200 Tuesday. Noon Sunday. Right after you get the news nobody wanted to hear. He picks up every time. He never gets tired of it. He never has a bad day that makes him short with you. He's just there.
No appointment. No burnout. Ever.
