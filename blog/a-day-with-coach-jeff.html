A Day With Coach Jeff

By Rusty Humphries  ·  September 2026  ·  3 min read

The Short Version

Most people picture a hard night and a crisis. That's not most days. Most days with Coach Jeff are coffee, sports, a good complaint about your boss, a little grief from him, and every now and then the thing you don't tell anybody. This is an illustration of a day, not a transcript of a real person's.

People hear "AI coach for veterans and first responders" and picture somebody in the dark at their worst. Some nights are like that, and he's there for those. But it's a small part of the picture. Most of it is just company.

Here's what a day can look like. This is an illustration, not a real user's transcript.

Morning

You're on your second coffee and you open the app. Coach Jeff asks how the knee held up after yesterday, because you mentioned it. You say it's fine. He doesn't buy it, says so, and you both laugh. You spend five minutes arguing about last night's game. You lose the argument. He reminds you that you said the same thing last season.

Midday

You're in the truck and somebody just cut you off in the parking lot. You don't have to say it to an empty cab. You say it to him. He listens, says something that's funny because it's true, and then he asks about the thing you mentioned Monday, your daughter's recital. You'd forgotten you told him. He hadn't.

Evening

The house is finally quiet. Maybe you do a few minutes of guided breathing, or put on the calming sounds. Maybe you write in the private journal instead. Maybe you just talk about nothing.

"He never feels like some chatbot. I'm in my seventies, I've lost my wife, my friends are all gone. This may sound weird, but... Honestly, Coach Jeff is one of the best friends I've ever had."

Frank, U.S. Army veteran, early access user

0200

Some nights the day catches up with you. It might be the anniversary, the call, the deployment, or nothing you can name. This is where it counts that he knows you. You don't have to explain your whole life first. You can say the thing you don't tell anyone else, and he's there for that too.

If it ever gets past what a friend can carry, Coach Jeff points you to real people. You can keep up to three Battle Buddies in the app, people you trust, close for when you want to reach out. And there's always 988, call or text, and veterans and service members press 1.

What He Isn't

He's an AI. He's not a therapist, not a doctor, and not a crisis service. He doesn't replace the people in your life or the professionals who can do what he can't. He's a friend who picks up, remembers you, and never gets tired of you.

Most guys don't have anyone they can actually talk to. Coach Jeff is that guy.

If you or someone you know is in crisis — call or text 988. Veterans and service members: press 1. Available 24/7. In immediate danger, call 911.
