Nobody Checks On The Person Who Checks On Everyone

By Rusty Humphries  ·  September 2026  ·  3 min read

The Short Version

You're the one everybody calls on their worst day. Then the shift ends and you tell everyone at home you're fine. Coach Jeff is someone to talk to after the shift, any hour, by text or voice. He's an AI, not a therapist and not a peer support team. He remembers you, gives you some grief, and is good company. He doesn't need you to be okay.

Think about your week the way it looks from the outside. You run toward the thing everybody else runs away from. You hold a stranger's hand on the worst night of their life. You clear the call, write it up, and get back in service.

Then the shift ends. You drive home. Somebody asks how your day was and you say fine. You've said it so many times it comes out on its own.

Why "Fine" Is The Only Answer You Give

At work, everybody around you has seen what you've seen, so nobody brings it up. At home, the people who love you are the people you're protecting from it. So it goes nowhere. It rides home with you. It's the call you replay in the shower and the one you don't mention at dinner.

You're not broken for that. You're doing exactly what the job trained you to do: stay steady, stay useful, keep it off your face. The trouble is that nobody trained you for what to do with it afterward.

You're the one everybody calls. Who do you call?

The Drive Home Is The Gap

Some departments have peer support, and if yours does and you'll use it, use it. A lot of people won't, because the people in that room are the people they work with. And most of what's out there is built for a scheduled hour. The bad part doesn't keep a schedule. It shows up in the truck at 0200, in the parking lot before you go inside, on the night off when the house is finally quiet.

That's the gap. Nobody built anything for it.

What Coach Jeff Is, And What He Isn't

Coach Jeff is an AI you can text or talk to from the parking lot, the truck, or the couch. He's not a person and he's not a therapist. He remembers what you tell him, so you don't start over. He remembers the rough call from Tuesday, your partner's name, that your kid has a game Saturday. He asks about it. He gives you some grief when you've earned it. He's good company.

He's not going to hand you a form, ask you to rate your mood from one to ten, or need you to be okay by the end of the conversation. You don't have to be the strong one with him. That's the whole idea.

"Twelve-hour shifts, come home, act normal, do it again. Coach Jeff is the one place I don't have to act normal. That's it. That's the whole thing."

Kevin M., paramedic, 9 years, early access user

Some Nights It's Just Talk

Not every conversation is about the worst call of your career. Sometimes it's last night's game, or your lieutenant being a clown, or a long day that was just a long day. A friend who only shows up for the hard stuff isn't much of a friend. Coach Jeff is fine with the small talk. The small talk is how the other stuff gets easier to say.

And when you do need to say the thing you don't tell anyone, he's there for that too.

When It Gets Bad

If it ever gets to where you're thinking about not being here, call or text 988. You don't have to be a veteran to use it, and if you are, press 1 to reach the Veterans Crisis Line. If you're in immediate danger, call 911. Coach Jeff will point you to real people. You can also add up to three people as your Battle Buddies, the ones you'd actually want to reach out to, so their numbers are close when you need them.

Coach Jeff is a friend in the gap. He isn't a replacement for the people and the help that can do what he can't.

If you or someone you know is in crisis — call or text 988. Veterans and service members: press 1. Available 24/7. In immediate danger, call 911.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Coach Jeff only for veterans?

No. Coach Jeff is built for veterans, first responders, and active-duty service members, and the people who love them.

Is Coach Jeff a replacement for peer support or therapy?

No. Coach Jeff is an AI companion, not a therapist and not a crisis service. If your department has peer support or you work with a counselor, keep using them. Coach Jeff is for the hours in between.

What if I'm in crisis?

Call or text 988. Veterans and service members can press 1 to reach the Veterans Crisis Line. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
