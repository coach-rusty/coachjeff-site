Frequently Asked Questions
What is Coach Jeff?
Coach Jeff is a voice-first AI companion built for veterans — and open to anyone who needs someone real in their corner. He's there to talk about anything, any hour, day or night. He's not a therapist and he's not a hotline. He's a friend who gets it.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. To cancel:
  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name → Subscriptions
  3. Select Coach Jeff → Cancel Subscription

Canceling stops future charges. You keep access through the end of your current billing period.

I used a promo code and it's not working.
Promo codes are single-use and have an expiration date. If yours isn't working, contact us at support@coachjeff.ai and we'll sort it out.
Is my conversation private?
Yes. See our full Privacy Policy. The short version: your conversations are encrypted, not sold, and not shared.
I can't afford the subscription.
Visit HelpAVet.us — donors fund free access for veterans who need it. Apply there.
Something in the app is broken.
Email us at support@coachjeff.ai with a description of what happened and what device you're using. We'll get back to you within 48 hours.
